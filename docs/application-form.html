How to submit

Mail this completed application, including both sponsor testimonials, with a check for your first year's dues to: Kittery Point Yacht Club, Treasurer, PO Box 373, New Castle, NH 03854-0373. Checks are payable to Kittery Point Yacht Club. Your application is voted on at the next monthly Board of Directors meeting. Approved applicants immediately become Probationary Members with full privileges and are contacted by the Treasurer with the Board's decision. Following the probationary period, each new member purchases one Share of stock in the club.

Board meeting month when your application is consideredFirst year's dues
January through July100% of the annual dues
August75%

Annual dues and the Share Value are set by the membership each January. As last published, annual dues were $400 and the Share Value $450 (two- and three-month payment plans are available for the Share). The Share Value is refundable on resignation, less any balances owed, as new members join. Please confirm current amounts with the Treasurer before sending payment.
