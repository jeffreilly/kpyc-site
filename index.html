Interim website: new site coming

A new KPYC website is on the way

The club's full website went offline in July 2026 due to a service disruption beyond our control. Rather than restore the old site, the Board has approved a move to ClubExpress, a club management service used by yacht clubs across the country, and we are now purchasing and deploying it as the club's new website and member portal. It is expected to launch by early January 2027 and possibly as early as mid October 2026. Until then, this interim site carries the club's essential information: use the links above for membership, the launch, the clubhouse, docks and moorings, the sailing school, and club documents.

We appreciate your patience and look forward to seeing you on the water.
